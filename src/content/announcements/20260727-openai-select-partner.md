**Miami, FL, USA · July 27, 2026** · Vaiyu Solutions, an AI operationalization consultancy that
takes AI from architecture to production for organizations in regulated industries, today announced
that it has been named an OpenAI Select Partner within the OpenAI Partner Network.

The OpenAI Partner Network is a global program for partners to build, sell, and deliver AI solutions
with OpenAI. It brings together partners with deep industry expertise, delivery capabilities, and
customer relationships while equipping them with resources, enablement, and support to help
enterprises adopt OpenAI frontier models and products and turn them into measurable impact.

As an OpenAI Select Partner, Vaiyu Solutions will continue working with OpenAI to help organizations
build, deploy, and scale AI solutions responsibly and effectively. This work will help organizations
get more useful work from every token and stronger performance per dollar with GPT-5.6, while using
ChatGPT Work to turn ambitious goals into finished work. Vaiyu Solutions specializes in environments
where a wrong answer carries regulatory, clinical, or safety consequences. Anchored in healthcare and
life sciences, and extending to financial services, energy and utilities, and manufacturing, the
company supports the full path from data engineering and model development through deployment,
MLOps, and the governance and validation evidence those environments demand.

> Being named an OpenAI Select Partner recognizes a specific kind of work: getting AI into production
> in environments where it has to withstand an auditor, an ethics board, and a long operational
> lifecycle. We work inside production environments across regulated organizations where AI adoption
> is gated not on model capability but on governance, evaluation, and deployment topology. Working
> with OpenAI, we intend to bring frontier models into those environments in a way that meets their
> demands: acceptance criteria established before development begins, evals as a merge gate, a named
> human accountable for every high-stakes decision, and a deployment posture that matches the
> regulatory regime.
>
> **Sarthak Pati, Ph.D.**, Founder & CEO, Vaiyu Solutions

Vaiyu Solutions supports organizations across healthcare, life sciences, and other regulated sectors,
including financial services, energy and utilities, and manufacturing, with discovery and AI
strategy, data engineering, model development and training, deployment and MLOps, AI governance and
compliance readiness, and fractional AI leadership.

Its work includes integrating an open-weight medical foundation model directly into a large health
system's radiology PACS, raising the quantitative measures available to oncologists at the point of
decision from one to five per patient, to 70–85, entirely inside the health system's perimeter, with
no patient imaging leaving the environment; optimizing a venture-backed oncology company's CT and PET
model for deployment in its customers' systems, cutting inference cost by 70% and moving the
non-accelerated CPU path from roughly 40 minutes to 12 per patient with model decisions unchanged;
and building a patient-identification model for a large pharmaceutical company's clinical trial
selection while rebuilding its training pipeline, cutting each training cycle from more than a week
to under four days. Vaiyu's founder was the first author on the largest federated learning study
conducted to date, published in
[Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-33407-5): 71 healthcare institutions
across six continents, 25,256 MRI scans from 6,314 patients, no record leaving its home institution,
and a 33% improvement over a publicly trained model in delineating the surgically targetable tumor.

Looking ahead, Vaiyu Solutions plans to build OpenAI-based offerings for regulated deployment:
reference architectures for data-residency-constrained topologies, evaluation harnesses that help
organizations safely deploy model upgrades, and human-oversight designs for high-stakes decisions,
and to scale these into healthcare, life sciences, and critical-infrastructure accounts, helping
customers translate AI ambition into business outcomes.

Learn more about the [OpenAI Partner Network](https://openai.com/business/partners/).
